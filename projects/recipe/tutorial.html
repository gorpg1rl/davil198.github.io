instructions



follow these simple steps to make your own one-page zine!

  1. gather (1) 8.5 by 11 sheet of paper, and a pair of scissors.
  2. fold the sheet of paper in half lengthwise. then press the fold to make a sharp crease with your finger.
  3. fold the sheet of paper in half again, this time crosswise. then press down to form a crease across the fold.
  4. fold in half once more, crosswise again. press down on the crease.
  5. unfold the sheet of paper to reveal all of your creases, then fold it in half crosswise.
  6. use your scissors to cut half way down the centre of the paper, starting on the side with the fold.
  7. unfold the sheet of paper once again.
  8. fold your sheet in half lengthwise, then hold the two ends of the paper and push them towards each other to form a plus sign.
  9. press down on all of the creases to ensure a flat and functional zine.
  10. enjoy!!! fill your zine however you'd like!!!
